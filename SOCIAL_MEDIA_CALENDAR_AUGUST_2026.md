FAQ schema, HowTo schema, and Article schema are now table stakes. Without them, AI engines skip your page entirely.

AI engines need precise definitions. Define every technical term you use.

📌 **Checklists Beat Paragraphs**
"7 steps to..." gets extracted. Wall of text gets skipped.
