## The Launch Day Playbook

### Pre-Launch (T-7 Days)
- [ ] Create 12 draft listings on Product Hunt (don't publish yet)
- [ ] Write your **Maker Comment** for each (see template below)
- [ ] Prepare 3-5 images/screenshots per tool (PNG, 2400×1600 ideal)
- [ ] Make a 60-second GIF/demo for the hero image
- [ ] Line up your "first voters" — friends, team, community (message them the night before)
- [ ] Post in relevant subreddits/Discords: "Launching on PH tomorrow, would love support"

### Launch Day (T-0)
- [ ] Publish at **12:01 AM PST** (catches US West + Europe + Asia cycle)
- [ ] Post your Maker Comment immediately (this is 30% of your engagement)
- [ ] Share on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit, IndieHackers, Hacker News
- [ ] Reply to EVERY comment within 15 minutes (PH algorithm rewards engagement speed)
- [ ] Update your product images/hunter if you get good feedback
- [ ] Track: upvotes, comments, traffic to developersmatrix.com

### Post-Launch (T+1 to T+7)
- [ ] Thank every commenter personally
- [ ] Fix bugs reported in comments immediately (show you're responsive)
- [ ] Share results on social ("We hit #3 Product of the Day! Here's what we learned")
- [ ] Add PH badge to your homepage
- [ ] Email your list (if you have one) about the launch
