## Part 8: GEO Optimization — AI Citation Engine

### What Makes AI Systems Cite Content (Priority Order)

1. **Direct answers in first 100 words** — H2 "Quick Answer" section
2. **Tables** — Comparison tables, feature tables, pricing tables
3. **Definitions** — Styled definition blocks with `<dfn>` or explicit format
4. **Timelines** — "History of X" or "2026 Updates" sections
5. **Checklists** — Numbered step-by-step with checkboxes
6. **Frameworks** — Named models or methodologies (e.g., "STAR Method")
7. **Original insights** — Data, stats, or analysis not found elsewhere
8. **Updated year references** — "2026" in title, H1, and body
9. **Strong semantic structure** — Proper heading hierarchy, semantic HTML
10. **FAQ schema** — 8–10 questions per page, directly answerable

### AI Citation Content Template

Every new blog post MUST follow this structure:

```
H1: [Exact question people ask AI] — 2026
H2: Quick Answer (1–2 paragraphs, direct answer, 40–60 words)
H2: What Is [Topic]? (Definition block)
H2: Why [Topic] Matters in 2026 (Context + stats)
H2: [Topic] Comparison Table (table with 3–5 columns)
H2: How [Topic] Works (Step-by-step, numbered)
H2: Key Benefits / Features (Bulleted list)
H2: Examples / Use Cases (Numbered, with real data)
H2: Expert Analysis (EEAT signal — original insight)
H2: FAQ (8–10 questions, H3 for each)
H2: Key Takeaways (3–5 bullet points)
H2: Sources (3–5 outbound links to .edu/.gov/major publications)
```

### Schema Requirements for GEO

Every page must have:
- ✅ `ArticleSchema` (with `author`, `datePublished`, `dateModified`)
- ✅ `BreadcrumbSchema`
- ✅ `FAQSchema` (8–10 questions)
- ✅ `HowToSchema` (for instructional content)
- ✅ `Person` schema for author
- ✅ `Organization` schema (enhanced with `founder`, `knowsAbout`)
